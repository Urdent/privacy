Privacy Policy

Klötzchen Bau Autopilot – last updated 8 November 2026

Klötzchen Bau Autopilot is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos of an animated miniature construction series on the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, schedule publication and read view statistics of the owner's own videos.

What is stored

The access token from Google is stored only on the owner's own computer. Video files, texts and statistics stay on that computer. No personal data of viewers or other people is collected, stored or processed.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data is sent only to YouTube (Google) to perform the uploads described above.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
